Privacy Statement

WFDC Wangnoi internal tools · Last updated 29 October 2026

Who these tools are for

The web apps hosted under berstream.github.io are internal tools built and run by the IT team of the WFDC Wangnoi warehouse (Thailand) for its own employees and contractors. They are not public services and do not imitate any other website.

What we collect

  • Employee ID and name — to identify who records a task.
  • Internal account password or PIN — for accounts created by our IT team for these tools only. We never ask for passwords of any other website, email or bank account.
  • GPS location and a photo at check-in — Agency Attendance only, to confirm the worker is in the warehouse building during the shift.
  • Work photos — of tasks, equipment and stock, as evidence for audits, asset checks and support tickets.

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank details or government ID numbers.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to run warehouse operations. It is stored in the services our tools run on (Google Apps Script / Google Sheets, Google Firebase, Supabase). It is not sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for advertising. Access is limited to the warehouse team and IT administrators.

Your choices

Employees can ask the WFDC Wangnoi IT team to view, correct or delete their records.
